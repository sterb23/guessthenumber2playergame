HOW TO PLAY

The Core Objective

You and your teammate each see a grid of 20 numbers. Out of all 20 numbers, EXACTLY ONE number exists on both of your boards at the same time!

Shared Lives & Turns

  • You and your partner share 3 Hearts.
  • Players take turns calling out or clicking a number.
  • Wrong Guess: Loses 1 Heart and turn goes to partner.

Communication Strategy

Group numbers into low/high ranges. Example: "I have lots of numbers in the 80s, do you?" Use the chat to narrow it down quickly!
