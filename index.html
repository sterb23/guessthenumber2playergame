HOW TO PLAY

The Core Objective

You and your teammate each see a grid of 20 numbers. Out of all 20 numbers, EXACTLY ONE number exists on both of your boards at the same time!

Shared Lives & Turn Taking

  • You and your partner share 3 Hearts.
  • Players take turns calling out or clicking a number.
  • Correct Guess: Instant Victory for both players!
  • Wrong Guess: Loses 1 Heart and hands turn over to partner.

Communication is Key

Call out 2-digit numbers to your partner in chat or out loud. Read numbers in groups (e.g., "Do you have 42, 88, or 19?") to narrow down the target fast.

Desktop & Mobile Shortcuts

  • Desktop: Hit Enter to type in chat instantly; press Esc to focus back on the board.
  • Mobile: Features native haptic vibration feedback for every tap, error, and victory!
